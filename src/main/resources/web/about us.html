About the Website

SkillMatch is an AI-powered system that scans a user's DOCX or PDF resume to identify skills, education, and experience, then matches them with suitable tech-related jobs and internships. It ranks opportunities based on compatibility, explains each recommendation, identifies skill gaps, and suggests relevant training to help users improve their qualifications.

It supports SDG 4 (Quality Education) by identifying skill gaps and recommending learning steps, SDG 8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth) by helping users find suitable employment, and SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities) by narrowing the information gap between individuals and available opportunities.
